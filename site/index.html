    keep going
    01 / the gap

    Two hours between classes is not a break. It's an unspent ticket.

    You were going to sit in the library and look at your phone. Instead: walk eight minutes, open a door, and sit at the back of a third-year lecture on something you will never otherwise learn. Nobody takes attendance in a room of two hundred.

    02 / the trick

    Work in a stranger's lecture and make the professor your background music.

    Bring the laptop. Do your actual readings. But instead of the hum of a café you get a live human being narrating Technology in the Modern World at you for an hour. When your brain gives out on your own work, you look up and learn something for free. Worst-case you got a quiet desk.

    03 / the point

    Knowledgemaxxing is real and your tuition is already spent.

    You bought access to the entire institution and you have been using roughly five courses of it. Every lecture you walk into is one you already paid for. The optimal number of random lectures attended before graduation is not zero. It is, conservatively, quite a lot.

    son·der

    n. the realization that each passer‑by has a life as vivid and complex as your own — every one of them on their way to a room you've never been in.

    That's the whole idea. Not scheduling. The vertigo.
